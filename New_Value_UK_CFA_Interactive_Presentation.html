04 / Proposed Research Extension

Design controlled experiments—before adding new models.

PROPOSED ONLY · NOT PERFORMED
EXPERIMENT 01 · VALIDITY

Point-in-time disclosure

Compare fiscal-year alignment with genuine public release-date information.

Hold constant: CBOP formula, universe, formation rule.
Measure: Spread and alpha.
EXPERIMENT 02 · EXECUTION

Cost-aware investability

Compare gross long–short with net returns under execution, borrowing, and financing costs.

Hold constant: Signals and dates.
Measure: Net return, capacity, drawdown.
EXPERIMENT 03 · MECHANISM

Accounting contribution

Compare B/M, OP/M, and CBOP/M, with industry-neutral robustness.

Hold constant: Eligible stocks and sorts.
Measure: OOS spreads, incremental alpha.

Pre-specify test periods and constraints. Report negative results. No invented backtest curves, risk metrics, or costs.

Technical Appendix · A1

Factor construction and regression notation.

PORTFOLIO FACTORS
HML = ½ (BH + SH − BL − SL)
NVF = ½ (BV + SV − BG − SG)
SMB = ⅓ (SMBHML + SMBRMW + SMBCMA)

B/S = big/small; H/L = high/low; V/G = value/growth in the relevant independent sort.

PRICING AND SPANNING
NVFt = α + β′Ft + εt
Ri,t = λ0,t + Σ λk,t β̂i,k + ηi,t
λ̂k = (1/T) Σ λ̂k,t

β: return exposure; α: conditional intercept; λ: cross-sectional risk price.

Factor mean, factor alpha, and estimated factor risk price are three different estimands.

TECHNICAL APPENDIX · A3/5Paper Tables 7 and 11A3
Technical Appendix · A4

Defense sheet: five questions worth being ready for.

Why is NVF mean not 1.175%?

2×3 factor versus 10-decile spread. Different portfolio constructions imply different returns.

Does alpha prove profit after costs?

No. Alpha controls selected pricing factors; it does not deduct turnover, borrow, or execution costs.

Is HML now obsolete?

Conditional inferential redundancy in selected UK tests does not imply universal irrelevance.

Does GRS p = .620 validate the model?

It means failing to reject joint zero alphas on the specified 25 test assets—not proof of model truth.

If you could fund one next experiment, which would you choose?

Point-in-time reconstruction followed by cost-aware out-of-sample evaluation: ensure the signal was available when traded and survived realistic implementation assumptions.

Technical Appendix · A5

GRS is a joint restriction test—not a trading rule.

GIBBONS–ROSS–SHANKEN TEST · SCHEMATIC FORM
GRS = T − N − KN × α̂′ Σ̂⁻¹ α̂1 + F̄′ Ω̂⁻¹ F̄   ∼   F(N, T − N − K)

Inputs

T = 288 months; N = 25 test portfolios; K factors; α̂ = vector of model alphas; Σ̂ = residual covariance; F̄ = mean factor returns; Ω̂ = factor covariance.

Interpretation

H₀: all test-asset intercepts equal zero. A low p-value is evidence against joint zero alphas, conditional on the model and test assumptions.

Supported: Compared on the same 25 size–B/M portfolios, replacing HML with NVF improves the reported GRS diagnostics.
Not supported: A higher p-value alone proves that a factor model is true or that a trading portfolio earns net alpha.
