Four-Pillar Falsification Framework: Time-Series, Cross-Section & Frontier Tests

Section 3.2 & Section 4 • Pages 5–7, 14–18
PILLAR 1: MULTI-FACTOR TIME-SERIES ALPHA (5 MODELS) TABLES 4, 7 & 11
$$\text{FF6: } R_{p,t} - R_{f,t} = \alpha_p + \beta_1(R_{m,t}-R_{f,t}) + \beta_2\text{SMB}_t + \beta_3\text{HML}_t + \beta_4\text{RMW}_t + \beta_5\text{CMA}_t + \beta_6\text{WML}_t + \varepsilon_{p,t}$$ $$\text{Fletcher (2021): } R_{p,t} - R_{f,t} = \alpha_p + \beta_1\text{MKT}_t + \beta_2\text{SMB}_t + \beta_3\text{HML}_t + \beta_4\text{WML}_t + \beta_5\text{INV}_t + \varepsilon_{p,t}$$
• Why Fletcher (2021) for the UK? In UK equities, asset growth ($\text{CMA}$) is weak ($t=1.425$). Fletcher replaces $\text{RMW}$ and $\text{CMA}$ with financing-based investment factors: Composite Share Issuance ($\text{CSI}$) and Net Share Issuance ($\text{NSI}$).
• Decision Rule: If $\alpha_{\text{High}-\text{Low}} > 0$ and $t(\alpha) > 2.58$, abnormal return cannot be explained by existing risk premia.
PILLAR 2: FACTOR SPANNING REGRESSIONS (FAMA-FRENCH, 2018) TABLE 9
$$\text{NVF}_t = \alpha_{\text{span}} + \sum_{k=1}^{K} \gamma_k F_{k,t} + u_t \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \Delta\left(\text{Max Sharpe}^2\right) = \frac{\alpha_{\text{span}}^2}{\sigma_u^2}$$
• Tests whether $\text{NVF}$ lies inside the linear span of $\{\text{MKT}, \text{SMB}, \text{HML}, \text{RMW}, \text{CMA}, \text{WML}, \text{CSI}, \text{NSI}\}$. A statistically significant intercept $\alpha_{\text{span}} > 0$ proves $\text{NVF}$ strictly expands the tangency portfolio's Sharpe ratio.
PILLAR 3: FAMA-MACBETH (1973) CROSS-SECTIONAL REGRESSIONS TABLE 10
$$\text{Step 2 (Each month } t\text{): } R_{i,t} - R_{f,t} = \lambda_{0,t} + \lambda_{\text{NVF},t}\hat{\beta}_{i,\text{NVF}} + \lambda_{\text{HML},t}\hat{\beta}_{i,\text{HML}} + \sum_{k} \lambda_{k,t}\hat{\beta}_{i,k} + \eta_{i,t}$$ $$\hat{\lambda}_k = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} \lambda_{k,t}, \qquad t(\hat{\lambda}_k) = \frac{\hat{\lambda}_k}{\text{SE}(\lambda_{k,t}) / \sqrt{T}}$$
• Runs across all individual stocks $i$ rather than aggregated portfolios, preventing loss of cross-sectional information and running a direct horse-race between $\hat{\beta}_{i,\text{NVF}}$ and $\hat{\beta}_{i,\text{HML}}$.
PILLAR 4: GIBBONS-ROSS-SHANKEN (GRS, 1989) EFFICIENCY TEST TABLE 12
$$\text{GRS} = \left(\frac{T}{N}\right)\left(\frac{T - N - L}{T - L - 1}\right) \left[\frac{\hat{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}' \hat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1} \hat{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}}{1 + \bar{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_f' \hat{\boldsymbol{\Omega}}^{-1} \bar{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_f}\right] \sim F_{N,\, T-N-L}$$
• Evaluates $N=25$ Size-Value portfolios. Null Hypothesis $H_0: \alpha_1 = \dots = \alpha_{25} = 0$.
• Crucial Interpretation: A smaller $\text{GRS}$ statistic and larger $p\text{-value}$ ($p > 0.05$) mean joint pricing errors are closer to zero (superior factor model).
METHODOLOGICAL RIGOR: Each econometric test addresses a distinct dimension—Portfolio Alpha (Pillar 1), Factor Redundancy (Pillar 2), Stock-Level Risk Premia (Pillar 3), and Joint Frontier Fit (Pillar 4).

"On Slide 4, we formalize the four econometric tests used to validate CBOP/M. First, we run multi-factor time-series regressions against five asset pricing models, including Fletcher's (2021) UK-specific 5-factor models that incorporate Composite Share Issuance (CSI) and Net Share Issuance (NSI).

SECTION 4 • CONSTRUCTIVE CRITIQUE & METHODOLOGICAL BOUNDARIES

Five Structural Limitations: Accounting Paradoxes, Benchmark Design & Market Frictions

Critical Peer Review of Le Quy Duong (2025)
1. The Intangible Capital Paradox (Internal Logic Contradiction)
Pages 1–2 critique $B/M$ for failing to capture intangible investments in the knowledge economy. Yet, $\text{OP} = \text{Rev} - \text{COGS} - \mathbf{\text{SG\&A}}$ deducts 100% of $\text{SG\&A}$, which houses R&D, software, IT, and human capital investment! Subtracting full $\text{SG\&A}$ penalizes asset-light knowledge firms and misclassifies R&D-heavy innovators as "low-cash-profitability" shorts (Peters & Taylor, 2017; Arnott et al., 2021).
2. Weak $\text{RMW}$ Benchmark (Footnote 3) & Missing $\text{OP}/M$ Horse-Race
• Footnote 3 (p. 5): Defines $\text{RMW}$ using Gross Profit Margin ($\text{GP}/\text{Revenue}$) instead of Fama-French (2015) Operating Profitability ($\text{OP}/\text{BE}$) or Novy-Marx (2013) $\text{GP}/\text{Total Assets}$. Margin conflates sector pricing power with capital efficiency, making $\text{RMW}$ artificially weak in Table 10 ($t = 0.218$).
• Missing Decomposition: Since $\frac{\text{CBOP}}{M} \equiv \frac{\text{OP}}{M} - \frac{\Delta\text{WC}}{M}$, not testing against $\text{OP}/M$ leaves unproven how much alpha stems from cash adjustment ($\Delta\text{WC}$) vs. simply replacing $B$ with flow earnings ($\text{OP}$).
3. Unconditional Sorting & Unintended Industry Bets
Sorting all non-financial firms unconditionally ignores structural working-capital differences across UK industries (e.g., Retail runs negative working capital via high payables $\Delta\text{AP}$, whereas Manufacturing carries heavy inventory $\Delta\text{INV}$).
4. Econometric Inference in Fama-MacBeth (EIV & Serial Correlation)
Table 10 runs second-stage regressions on individual stocks using first-stage estimated betas ($\hat{\beta}_{i,k}$). This introduces severe Errors-in-Variables (EIV) attenuation bias and requires Shanken (1992) standard error corrections and Newey-West HAC adjustments not reported in the paper. Also, setting missing working-capital components to zero (p. 4) risks measurement noise in early sample years.
5. Live Execution Frictions, UK Stamp Duty & 11-Month Signal Lag
• UK Stamp Duty Reserve Tax (SDRT): Every UK equity purchase incurs an immediate 50 bps ($0.50\%$) tax plus bid-ask spreads.
• Short-Leg Fragility: 50.4% of the raw spread ($-0.592\%$ of $1.175\%$) comes from shorting Decile 10 cash-burning firms ($\text{CBOP}/M = -0.454$), which face high stock-loan borrowing fees ($150–400\text{ bps/yr}$) and short-squeeze risk.
• Stale Annual Rebalancing: Rebalancing only once a year in July leaves accounting signals stale for up to 11–15 months.
$$\text{Untested Identity: } \underbrace{\frac{\text{CBOP}_{i,t}}{M_{i,t}}}_{\text{Paper's Metric}} = \underbrace{\frac{\text{OP}_{i,t} + \theta_{\text{int}}\text{SG\&A}_{i,t}}{M_{i,t}}}_{\text{Intangible-Adjusted Earnings Yield}} - \underbrace{\frac{\Delta \text{Working Capital Accruals}_{i,t}}{M_{i,t}}}_{\text{Sloan (1996) Cash Accrual Component}}$$
REVIEWER VERDICT: The study's core signal ($\text{CBOP}/M$) is theoretically sound and empirically strong, but requires 5 targeted quantitative upgrades to survive institutional live trading.
